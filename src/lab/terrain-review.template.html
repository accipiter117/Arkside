Terrain review

Proposed tile types for more varied maps, built the way the game builds its props (procedural, one tile each). Filter by district, flip day and night, drag to turn the board. Nothing here is in a map yet: each card says the gameplay rule it would carry.

Rules are proposals in gameplay terms. Cover: none, half (one tier) or full (two tiers). Sight: whether the tile blocks line of sight. Move: blocked, open, slow (2 moves), vault (hop in a straight line), climb (lights and mediums step up), ladder, ramp, narrow (no heavies), hack (opens on a hack).
